## Duo layout

The clock digits in `app/assets/duo-clock/` are original drawings, generated by `scripts/bake-clock.py` without a font file, and are covered by the project's MIT licence.

The sun position uses the low-precision formulas published by [Astronomy Answers](https://www.aa.quae.nl/en/reken/zonpositie.html), the same source the [SunCalc](https://github.com/mourner/suncalc) library credits. No SunCalc code is included.

The wallpapers in `duo-pack/themes/` are derived from the credited photographs above: each folder's `theme.json` names its author and photo page, and the wallpapers, night versions, frost, thumbnails and glass built from them retain those terms. The tiles in `duo-pack/icons/` are built from the marks listed under Icons and trademarks, or are plain initials set in Manrope.

The glass is rendered once, on a computer, by [webgl-apple-liquid-glass](https://github.com/Oliverrr2424/webgl-apple-liquid-glass) by Oliver Nemo. A copy of its source is in `scripts/duo-pack/renderer/` under its MIT licence, retained there as `LICENSE`. It is a development tool and is not copied into the TV package.

A wallpaper an owner adds for themselves stays in ignored `.local/` and only enters that owner's personal build. It is never part of the source, the public export or this repository's history.

## Sounds

Verre, Goutte, Galet, Bois, Feutre, Bronze and Ardoise in `app/assets/sounds/` are computed by `scripts/make-sounds.py`, without any outside recording, and are covered by the project's MIT licence.

Zen is derived from the Zen pack of [UI SFX](https://github.com/romainsimon/uisfx) 0.4.0, whose audio is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. `scripts/make-sounds.py` matches its loudness and tunes its click along the scale. The licence text is `app/assets/sounds/LICENSE-AUDIO`.

## Development dependencies and exports

Development dependencies retain the licences in their npm packages. They are not copied into the TV JavaScript runtime.

`npm run export:public` excludes private fonts and includes this notice and the root MIT licence. Preserve the included font and icon licence texts. Review [public release preparation](docs/public-release.md) before publishing a repository or download.
